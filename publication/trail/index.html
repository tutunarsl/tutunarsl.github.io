TRaIL-Odom: Tightly Coupled Continuous Time Radar-IMU-LiDAR Odometry With Adaptive Doppler Weighting

Chiyun Noh, Turcan Tuna, William Talbot, Marco Hutter, Laurent Kneip, Ayoung Kim
2026 IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L), accepted 23 August 2026
PDF Cite Project Code

Overview

Radar–IMU–LiDAR odometry adjusts individual Doppler constraints and overall radar weight according to weakly constrained LiDAR directions. Evaluated on 13 sequences.

Accepted 23 August 2026; assigned to the November 2026 issue, 11(11), 12631–12638. Status recorded as of 22 October 2026.
